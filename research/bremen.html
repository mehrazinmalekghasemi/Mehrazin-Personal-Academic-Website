Research 01 · University of Bremen · Jul 2026 — Present

Fairness in
Online Scheduling

I study fairness notions in scheduling settings such as interval scheduling, and investigate whether learning-augmented algorithms and randomization can improve approximation guarantees.

What I am exploring

The project combines fairness with online and learning-augmented algorithmic techniques. A central direction is understanding how predictions about the input can be used without sacrificing rigorous guarantees, and how randomization may help overcome deterministic limitations.
