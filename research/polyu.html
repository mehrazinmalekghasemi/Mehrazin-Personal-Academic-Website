Research 03 · The Hong Kong Polytechnic University · Jul — Sep 2025

Graphical
Resource Allocation

During my on-site research internship at PolyU, I investigated fairness and efficiency in resource allocation when agents' feasible bundles are constrained by a graph.

What I worked on

I studied the compatibility of non-wastefulness and envy-freeness up to one good (EF1) under independent-set allocation constraints, using graph-theoretic structure to reason about feasible allocations.
