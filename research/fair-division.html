Fair Allocation
& Market Design

I work on fair allocation of indivisible resources and related market-design questions, studying fairness guarantees, competitive equilibrium, and the structure of fair allocations.

What I am exploring

My work includes studying recent literature in fair allocation and market design, analyzing competitive equilibrium in Fisher markets, investigating the existence of MMS and EFX allocations in general settings, and exploring fairness properties of newer settings and definitions.
