04 / About

Why algorithms?

I am a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Sharif University of Technology, interested in the mathematical side of computing and in problems where algorithmic choices affect people, resources, or collective decisions.

My mathematics olympiad background shaped my interest in problem solving. Research in fair division and algorithmic game theory led me toward broader questions in optimization and algorithms. I also enjoy computational geometry and the way geometric structure can make an abstract problem visual, precise, and elegant.
